Safety of Intelligent Vehicles

This undergraduate general education elective traces the development of automotive safety from passive crash protection to intelligent safety systems. It introduces the scientific foundations, research methods, and emerging challenges of intelligent vehicle safety and future mobility.

Course Overview

This course introduces the scientific foundations, current research, and future directions of intelligent vehicle safety. Students explore how safety technologies protect lives and shape future mobility, while building a foundation for understanding transportation safety. The course also examines the contributions of science, technology, and industry to society and encourages students to develop the confidence and skills to innovate responsibly. Course content includes:

Foundations

An overview of automotive safety and occupant restraint systems

Future Trends

Intelligent vehicles, future mobility, and transportation safety

Current Research

Our team's research on intelligent vehicle safety and electric vehicle battery thermal runaway prevention, illustrated through incident case studies and recent findings

Prerequisites

  • No formal prerequisites
  • Recommended for students in their second year or later

Course Schedule

  • Week 1
    IntroductionAn introduction to the course, road traffic, and intelligent vehicles; the scope of intelligent vehicle safety; and key research questions, methods, trends, and case studies in automotive safety.
  • Week 2
    Overview of Automotive SafetyAn introduction to active and passive safety through practical examples of vehicle safety challenges.
  • Week 3
    Occupant Restraint SystemsFundamentals of occupant restraint systems, simplified analysis of crash dynamics, and principles of restraint system design.
  • Week 4
    Research Methods in Automotive SafetyExperimental and numerical methods, human surrogates for safety research, and the role of active occupant responses in vehicle safety.
  • Week 5
    Field Visit: Intelligent Mobility in PracticeAn off-campus field study, tentatively planned in Yizhuang with Luobo Chuxing, Baidu, or Qingzhou Chuxing.
  • Week 6
    Road User BehaviorAn introduction to different road user groups and intelligent vehicle cockpits, with a discussion of travel preferences.
  • Week 7
    Electric Vehicle SafetyA guest lecture on electric vehicle technologies and key safety concerns, including battery crash safety and protective measures.
  • Week 8
    Thermal Runaway Protection for Electric VehiclesCase studies of electric vehicle incidents, thermal runaway protection for traction batteries, and prediction of safety risks following battery damage.
  • Week 9
    Operational Safety of Intelligent VehiclesFoundations of operational safety, hazard identification, quantitative risk assessment, and decision-making and control for safe vehicle operation.
  • Week 10
    Intelligent Vehicle System SafetyFundamentals of intelligent vehicles, functional safety, safety of the intended functionality, information security, and the role of artificial intelligence in intelligent vehicle systems.
  • Week 11
    Automotive Safety Testing and EvaluationTest scenarios and tools, safety regulations, consumer safety assessment methods, and the implications of changes in the automotive industry for safety testing.
  • Week 12
    Immersive Laboratory SessionImmersive interaction in hazardous traffic scenarios.
  • Week 13
    Automotive Safety and Future MobilityA seminar on safety in connected and automated mobility, cooperation between vehicles and road infrastructure, advances in intelligent protection, and the role of intelligent vehicles in future transport systems and cities.
  • Week 14
    Automotive Safety and Future SocietyA seminar examining whether road traffic crashes can ever be eliminated and the ethical dilemmas in autonomous vehicle decision-making.
  • Week 15
    Course Project PresentationsGroup presentations and instructor feedback on the course projects.
