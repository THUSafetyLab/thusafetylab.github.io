First-Year Orientation Course

Designed primarily for first-year undergraduates, this course supports the transition to university through general education, scientific inquiry, and personal development. Its three modules focus on Finding Your Place at University, Exploring Your Field of Study, and Understanding Yourself. Through small-group seminars and hands-on activities, students discover university life and Tsinghua traditions, explore interdisciplinary research through intelligent vehicle case studies, and develop self-awareness, resilience, and a sense of responsibility.

Course Overview

This one-credit course comprises 16 hours across seven sessions. Small-group teaching combines classroom instruction, experiential learning, peer discussion, feedback, and student presentations. Three connected modules help students navigate university life, explore their academic interests, and reflect on their personal development:

Finding Your Place at University

Students learn how to navigate university study and campus life, discover Tsinghua's history, traditions, and culture, and build supportive relationships with faculty and peers.

Exploring Your Field of Study

Intelligent vehicles provide an accessible introduction to interdisciplinary research. Students explore technological advances, research and industry practice, and the process of scientific inquiry.

Understanding Yourself

Students reflect on their interests, strengths, values, and goals, develop resilience and strategies for adapting to university life, and consider the responsibilities associated with scientific and technological progress.

Course Schedule

Activities include classroom instruction, peer discussion, a visit to campus sports facilities with an introduction to fencing, research case studies, small-group discussions, and student presentations. Guest speakers and activities may vary from year to year; consult the current course announcements for confirmed arrangements.

  • Week 1
    Course Introduction and Getting to Know One AnotherAn introduction to the course structure and assessment, followed by stories of personal development, small-group icebreaker activities, and a discussion of students' expectations for university life.
  • Week 2
    Learning from Upper-Year StudentsInvited upper-year students share their experiences of academic study, research and innovation, student leadership, public service, sports, and campus life. The session encourages students to build confidence, explore their interests, and find an approach to university life that suits them.
  • Week 3
    North Gymnasium Visit and Introduction to FencingStudents visit Tsinghua University's North Gymnasium, explore campus sports facilities, and try fencing in an introductory session that brings physical education beyond the classroom.
  • Week 4
    Exploring Vehicle Engineering and Academic PathwaysUsing vehicle engineering as a case study, the session explores emerging technologies, industry and career pathways, the role of automobiles in society, and how practical engineering problems lead to research questions.
  • Week 5
    AI and Automotive Safety: A Research Case StudyA student research project on an AI-enabled active hood for pedestrian protection illustrates the research process, from identifying a safety problem and framing a research question to simulation, analysis, and interpretation of results.
  • Week 6
    Intelligent and Connected VehiclesAn introduction to the development and key concepts of intelligent and connected vehicles and levels of driving automation, followed by discussion of societal benefits, developments in China and internationally, and major technical and social challenges.
  • Week 7
    Student Presentations and ReflectionStudents present their interests, experiences, or personal reflections in a format of their choice, exchange ideas with classmates and instructors, and reflect on the course and their plans for university life.
