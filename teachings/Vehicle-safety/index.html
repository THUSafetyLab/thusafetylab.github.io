Vehicle Safety

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the scientific foundations, research methods, and emerging technologies of automotive safety. It covers interdisciplinary topics including vehicle engineering, numerical computation, artificial intelligence, and data analysis, with an emphasis on vehicle engineering.

Course Overview

This course addresses the safety needs of the human–vehicle–road system. Students will study the fundamental theories of automotive safety, learn protection methods and design applications, and develop interdisciplinary analytical skills. Course content includes:

Concepts and Frameworks

Fundamental concepts and frameworks of automotive safety

Methods

Classical methods and their recent developments

Research Topics

Specialized research topics in automotive safety

Interdisciplinary Research

Vehicle engineering, numerical computation, artificial intelligence, and data analysis

Prerequisites

  • Calculus
  • University Physics
  • Mechanics of Materials
  • Vehicle theory and control theory (recommended)

Course Schedule

  • Week 1
    IntroductionCourse information, the current state of road traffic safety, and the research subjects and methods of automotive safety.
  • Week 2
    Fundamental Concepts and Technologies of Automotive SafetyAn introduction to active and passive automotive safety technologies through events related to automotive safety.
  • Week 3
    Occupant Restraint SystemsFundamental concepts of occupant restraint systems, occupant collision dynamics, restraint system design principles, recent research on intelligent vehicle safety technologies, and future trends in automotive safety.
  • Week 4
    System Safety, Operational Safety, and Intelligent ProtectionFundamental concepts and guiding principles, safety decision-making for intelligent vehicles, system safety, operational safety, and intelligent protection.
  • Week 5
    Dimensional Analysis, Experimental Techniques, and Numerical MethodsDimensional analysis, experimental techniques and methods, virtual reality and accelerated sampling of rare hazardous scenarios, numerical research methods, and virtual simulation data collection.
  • Week 6
    Injury Biomechanics: Theories and MethodsInjury mechanisms and tolerance, injury risk assessment methods, human surrogates in automotive safety, injury protection in multiple fields, and the concepts and applications of active human response.
  • Week 7
    Safety Protection for Different Population GroupsChild occupant protection, occupant restraint system design, pedestrian protection, and related discussions and review.
  • Week 8
    Case Studies and DiscussionCase studies of severe high-speed rear-end collisions and battery safety, with an extended introduction to passive safety in formula racing.
  • Week 9
    Automotive Safety Testing and EvaluationTest scenarios and tools, regulations and consumer-oriented assessment methods, safety testing under the four transformations of the automotive industry, and battery safety evaluation.
  • Week 10
    Machine Learning Methods and Applications in Automotive SafetyFundamental concepts of artificial intelligence, commonly used machine learning algorithms, applications of machine learning in automotive safety, and related discussion and review.
  • Week 11
    Future Crash Scenarios and Risk QuantificationFuture road traffic crash scenarios, intelligent vehicle risk prediction, prediction-based active hood systems, autonomous vehicle decision-making with ethical intervention, and safety and trust.
