About the Faculty Team

The Automotive Safety and Lightweighting Research Team is academically led by Professor Zhou Qing, with Associate Researcher Xia Yong and Associate Professor Nie Bingbing jointly contributing to its academic supervision. The faculty members have long been actively engaged in research and education, and have established distinguished academic influence in the field of automotive safety both in China and internationally.

  • Research achievements. In recent years, the team has led more than 20 research projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, national key research and development programs, and international collaborative initiatives. The team has published over 100 high-quality academic papers and obtained nearly 100 granted invention patents in China and the United States. It has established long-term, stable collaborations with leading companies in the automotive industry, both domestically and internationally. Some of its core technologies have achieved internationally leading performance and have been implemented in mass production.
  • Academic leadership and influence. Faculty members have held prominent academic positions, including Council Member of the International Research Council on Biomechanics of Injury (IRCOBI), with the first Chinese scholar to serve in this capacity; head of the Pedestrian Safety Division of SAE International; Member of the Academic Committee of Tsinghua University; and Chair of the School Academic Committee. They have also served as associate editors or editorial board members of internationally recognized journals, including International Journal of Impact Engineering, Accident Analysis & Prevention, Traffic Injury Prevention, and SAE International Journals.
  • Education and mentoring. Faculty members teach a series of foundational courses on automotive crash safety. Their teaching achievements include recognition as one of the inaugural National First-Class Undergraduate Courses by the Ministry of Education of China, with enrollment exceeding 47,000 students; recognition for high-quality undergraduate teaching plans among Beijing universities; designation as Tsinghua University Excellent Courses; and recognition as Model Courses for Curriculum-Based Ideological and Political Education. Graduates have pursued careers at leading universities worldwide and in frontline positions across industry. Faculty members have received Tsinghua University's "Good Teacher and Helpful Friend" (良师益友) recognition on six occasions.
